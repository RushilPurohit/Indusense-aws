ABB INDUSTRIAL MOTOR M-102 MAINTENANCE & TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
Document ID: DOC-ABB-M102-V2
Section: 4.0 Maintenance & Operating Standards

1. OPERATING TEMPERATURE & THERMAL LIMITS
1.1 Normal Operating Range:
    - Ambient air temperature: 295 K - 303 K (22°C - 30°C)
    - Maximum process temperature limit: 312 K (39°C)
    - Winding temperature warning threshold: 358 K (85°C)

1.2 Thermal Overload Protection (HDF):
    - Heat Dissipation Failure (HDF) occurs when process temperature exceeds ambient by > 8.6 K and rotational speed is below 1380 RPM.
    - Recommended Inspection: Clean cooling fins, verify intake fan operation, check ventilation airflow.

2. TROUBLESHOOTING PROCEDURES FOR MOTOR OVERHEATING (ERROR E102)
2.1 Symptoms:
    - Motor housing temperature exceeds 80°C
    - Thermal sensor alarm E102 triggered on PLC dashboard
    - Increased acoustic noise and vibration in front bearing housing

2.2 Possible Causes:
    - Cause A: Blocked cooling fan intake or accumulated dust on cooling fins.
    - Cause B: Excessive rotational load / tool wear > 200 min leading to Overstrain Failure (OSF).
    - Cause C: Insufficient or degraded synthetic grease in drive-end bearing.

2.3 Recommended Action Sequence:
    Step 1: Safely halt unit M-102 and lock out electrical power.
    Step 2: Inspect intake fan shroud for foreign objects and clean cooling fins using compressed air.
    Step 3: Measure drive-end bearing temperature and inspect grease seal for leakage.
    Step 4: Check cutting tool wear level; replace cutting tool if wear exceeds 200 min limit.
    Step 5: Measure winding insulation resistance with megohmmeter before restarting.

3. PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE & SENSOR ALERTS
- Power Failure (PWF): Triggered when torque * rotational speed exceeds 9,000 W or falls below 110 W.
- Tool Wear Failure (TWF): Triggered when cumulative tool wear reaches 200-240 minutes.
